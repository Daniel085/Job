## Formatting Guidelines

**Length:**
- Target 20-30 lines (about 60% of a typical cover letter)
- Concise but impactful
- See `/examples/cover-letters/` - most are 27-29 lines

**Tone:**
- Professional but personable
- Confident without arrogance
- Enthusiastic about the company (not desperate)
- Use "I" statements backed by evidence

**What to Avoid:**
- Generic statements ("I'm a passionate PM...")
- Repeating your resume without new context
- Typos or company name errors (proofread!)
- Overly formal language ("I am writing to express my interest...")
- Missing metrics (always quantify when possible)

**Checklist Before Sending:**
- [ ] Company name and role title correct throughout
- [ ] Referenced something specific about the company
- [ ] Included 2-3 achievements with metrics
- [ ] Matched achievements to job requirements
- [ ] Kept to ~25 lines or less
- [ ] Proofread for typos
- [ ] Saved as `/cover-letters/cover-letter-[company].md`
