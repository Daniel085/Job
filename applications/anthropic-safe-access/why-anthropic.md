# "Why Anthropic?" answer for the Safe Access application

*Greenhouse prompt: "Why do you want to work at Anthropic? (We value this response highly - great answers are often 200-400 words.)" Draft below is 290 words. Every claim traces to Profile.md.*

---

I use Claude Code every day, and the product I work on exists because of the question Anthropic takes most seriously: what should an AI be allowed to do, and who decides.

At Alianza I own the developer-facing side of Crux, the control plane through which AI agents get access to live phone calls on more than a thousand operators' networks. The platform applies identity, policy, and consent in the network before any agent is invoked. My job is the surface where developers meet those controls: the portal, the quickstarts, the authentication guide, the MCP server. I have watched what happens when access rules are clear and when they are not. When they are clear, developers build inside them. When they are not, developers route around them. That is the reason the Safe Access role reads to me as the most consequential product at the company. It sits on the critical path of every launch, and the quality of its developer experience decides whether the controls hold.

I also have a view on what Claude Code and the API still need from the access side, because I live on both ends of it. I built a production documentation system with AI agents and had to decide, failure by failure, where to trust it. I wrote publicly about systems designed to make things up, and about keeping humans where humans add the value. That is not a pitch against the technology. It is the discipline I would bring to deciding how access is granted, verified, and revoked for it.

Anthropic is the one lab whose stated reason for existing matches the problem I have chosen to work on. I would rather help get the access layer right here, for every Claude surface, than keep solving it for one network at a time.
