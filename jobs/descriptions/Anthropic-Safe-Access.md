## About the role

Own the product strategy and experience for Anthropic's centralized identity, verification, and access system, which every Claude surface draws on. Described as a 0-to-1 product that sits on the critical path of every model launch and every access program.

## Responsibilities

- Own the product strategy and roadmap for account and access primitives
- Own the end-to-end experience of getting and managing access for internal teams and external customers and partners
- Support the API designs and UX of access APIs used by platforms, resellers, and internal services
- Partner with trusted access and verification PMs so new programs build on shared primitives
- Coordinate across product, safeguards, legal, and go-to-market teams

## Minimum qualifications

- Are a strong executor with a track record of shipping through ambiguity and cross-organization dependencies
- Have owned a developer platform, identity, authorization, or access-management product end to end
- Care about Anthropic's mission, specifically scaling access to Claude safely
- Years of experience not fixed: "we care about the ownership, not the years," though most people have five or more years as a PM

## Preferred qualifications

- 8+ years of product management experience
- Experience with multi-tenant platforms used by first-party apps, direct API customers, and third parties
- Built account, identity, or subscription systems spanning more than one product
- Took a product from zero to one
