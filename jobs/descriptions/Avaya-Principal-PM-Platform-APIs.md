## Scope

Help advance the platform and developer capabilities behind Avaya Infinity, the platform that "unifies fragmented customer experiences, connecting the channels, insights, technologies, and workflows." Someone who "thinks in platforms and understands how APIs, integrations and developer experiences can make enterprise software easier to extend, integrate and build on." Hands-on product leadership moving between customer problems, technical architecture, developer needs, and business outcomes.

## Responsibilities

- Shape product strategy and roadmap for platform, API, and developer capabilities
- Work with customers, partners, developers, and internal teams on integration and extensibility needs
- Assess existing capabilities; find opportunities to improve the developer and integration experience
- Define requirements for APIs, events, integrations, developer tooling, and related platform capabilities
- Partner with Engineering and Architecture on technical tradeoffs, sequencing, execution
- Translate complex enterprise requirements into reusable platform capabilities rather than one-off solutions
- Champion developers and partners as users of the platform
- Create more consistent integration patterns across Avaya product teams
- Define measures for adoption, developer success, platform usage, customer value
- Participate directly in customer and partner discovery

## Qualifications

- Strong PM experience with enterprise software platforms, APIs, developer products, cloud services
- Good understanding of modern APIs, authentication and authorization, events, integrations, cloud architecture
- Experience working closely with engineering and architecture on technically complex products
- Built products or capabilities used by developers, partners, system integrators, or enterprise technology teams
- Understand architecture and make product tradeoffs without being the implementing engineer
- Strong customer discovery and product judgment; turn an ambiguous problem into an executable roadmap
- Strong written and verbal communication

**Particularly valuable:** enterprise communications, CCaaS, CPaaS, developer platforms, AI platforms, enterprise integration, event-driven architectures, large-scale SaaS; "experience helping evolve an established enterprise product toward a more open and extensible platform."
