## About the role

The New Markets and Monetization team brings Claude to every business through the Claude Platform. Two pillars:

- **Verticals:** identifying and building for the next API markets
- **Monetization:** the commercial infrastructure for usage that flows through partners, resellers, and other platforms

Anthropic is hiring founding PMs who will each own one area, work with a dedicated engineering lead and designer, and be measured on company-level half-year goals.

## Areas (each PM owns one)

- **Partner and platform billing:** Metering, attribution, and billing for usage that reaches Claude through partners or resellers, plus the related program terms
- **Spend observability and controls:** Giving enterprise buyers clear spend visibility and limits they can enforce, including programmatically
- **One account:** A single login and wallet across the API and Claude subscriptions, for the startup path
- **A vertical:** Platform strategy for a specific industry, including which bets to make and how to ship them

All roles involve building on Billing Platform and Auth & Identity primitives, structuring commercial terms, engaging the whole ecosystem, owning outcome metrics, and writing strategy and specs.
