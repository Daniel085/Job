## Scope

Lead the evolution of Intuit's **internal** Agent Software Development Lifecycle (ADLC): "the paved path for how Intuit builds and deploys agents at scale." Bring together state-of-the-art agent harness capabilities and starter kits so thousands of internal builders can deploy secure, scalable AI agents.

## Responsibilities

- Define the multi-year vision, strategy, and roadmap for the internal Agent SDLC: the Agent Starter Kit (ASK), Agent Sandbox, and developer productivity tooling; scale active developer adoption and cut agent development and deployment time in half
- Diagnose pain points across independently built business-unit agent stacks, craft platform value propositions, pitch next-generation architectures, drive platform convergence
- Stitch together ADLC capabilities, context/memory architectures, and agent harness systems within the AI Platform team into cohesive developer products
- Monitor frontier trends across proprietary stacks (Claude/Anthropic SDKs, OpenAI ecosystems) and open-source agent frameworks; establish safe, pluggable, config-driven enterprise standards
- Partner with engineering, architecture, security, and business-unit leaders on technical trade-offs with an AI-first mindset

## Qualifications (not split into required and preferred)

- 8+ years PM delivering complex developer platforms, AI/ML infrastructure, or enterprise SDLC tools (or equivalent)
- Deep technical fluency in state-of-the-art agentic frameworks (LangGraph, Claude SDK, OpenAI SDK, open-source agent tooling), model context/memory architectures, and AI-native harness capabilities, used at scale
- Track record of navigating ambiguity, diagnosing friction in disparate systems, pitching platform products to internal stakeholders, driving multi-team platform convergence
- Strategic storytelling, boundaryless influence, leadership from granular technical requirements to platform architecture
