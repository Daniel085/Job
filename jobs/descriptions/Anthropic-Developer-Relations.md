## About the role

Help developers discover, onboard, and get the most out of Claude Code, Claude Tag, and future developer products. Gather developer feedback for product and research teams and promote responsible, safe, and secure use of Anthropic's products.

## Responsibilities

- **Technical strategy:** Shape how developers find and use Claude Code and Claude Tag, and build ways to measure developer success. Work with product and engineering so developer needs inform the roadmap.
- **Craft:** Help define good AI developer relations practice, and model it through technical content, code samples, and developer engagement.
- **Adoption:** Serve developers from hobbyists to enterprise teams, support GTM and marketing campaigns, lead events and workshops, and create tutorials, sample apps, documentation, and integration guides.
- **Cross-functional work:** Advocate for developers internally, turn their feedback into initiatives, work with Research on educational resources, and promote responsible AI practices.

## About you

- 5+ years of experience in developer relations, developer experience, or technical product/engineering roles
- Technically credible, able to explain AI/ML and API concepts to different audiences
- Able to write production-quality code demos and give actionable technical feedback on samples
- Understand developer behavior and how to reach developers and drive adoption
- Committed to responsible AI development and interested in AI safety and ethics
- Influence cross-functional teams without direct authority; adapt well in high-growth settings
