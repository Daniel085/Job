## Scope

Grows 1Password's new business in AI: decides which customers to target and which outcomes to win on, proves it with design customers, and scales through the go-to-market organization, the agent ecosystem, and channel partners.

## Responsibilities

**Growth strategy**
- Own the growth thesis for 1Password's AI opportunities (segments, outcomes, workflows to embed in); revise it as evidence arrives
- Lead a weekly business review combining voice of customer, field feedback, and usage telemetry
- Identify adoption blockers and drive each to resolution with the owning product team

**Design customer program**
- Run the design-customer program for new AI opportunities, from selection through delivered outcomes
- Act as "general contractor" for each design customer: the single person accountable for getting that customer deployed on 1Password for agentic access, working with a forward-deployed engineering team
- Turn what the program proves into the repeatable evidence, deployment pattern, and measured outcomes GTM and channel teams build sales plays on

**Partnerships**
- Decide where 1Password should sit in the customer's AI stack for securing agent access
- Identify and pursue partnerships that complete an end-to-end customer outcome; find integration patterns that repeat across similar partners
- Work with business development on commercial terms

**Scale through the field and developer community**
- Build demos and enablement content with product enablement for field and channel use
- Work with developer relations on reference architectures, sample code, and open-source contributions

## Requirements

- 8+ years PM experience, including carrying a revenue or adoption target; preference for security, agentic products, or developer tools
- Owned the strategy for a new product area or market entry, from hypothesis to customers in production
- Worked directly with enterprise customers on a technical deployment as the person accountable, and turned it into a repeatable sales motion
- Built technology partnerships and integrations, including judging where a product sits in a larger ecosystem and working with BD on commercial terms
- Technical depth to talk credibly with developers and security architects about how AI agents are built, deployed, and given access to systems
- Builds alignment across GTM and engineering without authority; decides with incomplete information; owns the work between teams
- Works AI-first: uses agents in daily work
- Preferred: familiarity with agentic access products (products that give AI agents credentials and access to systems)
