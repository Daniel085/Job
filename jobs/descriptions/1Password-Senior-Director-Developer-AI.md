## Scope

Leads product strategy and execution across the Developer & AI portfolio: Developer Experience (DevX) products, Security for AI, AI for Security, and the product surface that supports standards adoption and developer engagement. Defines vision, roadmap, and outcomes for how developers and AI-powered systems securely interact with 1Password, from local development and secrets management, to programmatic agent access, to secure runtime environments. Leads a senior group of product managers.

## Responsibilities

- Set and evolve the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for Developer Experience, AI security, and AI-powered security initiatives
- Lead product definition for secure, agentic workflows including identity, delegation, secrets delivery, and access control for both developers and AI agents
- Translate emerging AI capabilities and risks into durable, differentiated product primitives
- Work in lockstep with the VP of Developer & AI Engineering on technical vision, architecture, and delivery
- Partner with Marketing, Partnerships, Security, and GTM on launches, platform integrations, and ecosystem adoption
- Represent product strategy internally and externally across messaging, documentation, and developer engagement
- Provide product leadership into standards participation and developer advocacy
- Recruit, mentor, and lead senior product managers; establish operating rhythms, decision frameworks, and outcome metrics

## Requirements

- 10+ years in product management, including 5+ years leading senior PMs or product portfolios
- Experience owning platform, developer-facing, or infrastructure products strongly preferred
- Track record of shipping developer tools, platforms, or security products used by large or fast-growing audiences
- Strong familiarity with the modern AI landscape: LLMs, orchestration frameworks, agentic workflows
- Solid understanding of identity, secrets management, access control, and secure system design
- Comfort in highly technical problem spaces alongside senior engineers
- Experience balancing discovery, delivery, and platform investment; influence without authority
