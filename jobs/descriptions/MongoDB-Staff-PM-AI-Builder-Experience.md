## Scope

AI Builder Experience (ABX) is "MongoDB's front door for the agentic era." This role owns the Partner Platform: "the products, experiences, and operating model that allow ecosystem partners to build trusted, high-quality MongoDB experiences for their users. This includes the Partner API and the broader path toward self-serve integrations, partner onboarding, enterprise controls, marketplace/discovery, and partner-managed experiences." Explicitly "at the intersection of humans, machines, and partners": human builders work through AI agents, agents need reliable interfaces, context, permissions, and tools, and partners decide where those experiences are discovered.

## Responsibilities

- Own vision, strategy, and roadmap for the Partner Platform, starting with the Partner API and evolving toward a self-serve partner experience
- Build the next phases of the Partner API: self-serve onboarding, enterprise and administrator controls, partner-managed account creation and billing, partner discovery, a high-quality integration bar
- Establish a repeatable operating model for partner intake, design-partner engagement, integration support, launch readiness, documentation, incident response, post-launch learning
- Partner with the Partner team, IAM, Billing, Security, Engineering, Design, Docs, Analytics, Product Marketing, Sales, Technical Services
- Work directly with strategic AI-native partners and cloud platforms
- Ensure the platform works for both human and agentic developers: "APIs and tools must be understandable to people, usable by agents, observable, secure, and resilient under automation"
- Define metrics: partner activation, time to first successful integration, integration quality, end-user adoption, successful agentic workflows, cluster growth, partner-influenced revenue
- Represent ABX externally on AI-native product development, developer ecosystems, and partner platforms

## Requirements

- 8+ years PM or equivalent, including ownership of complex technical products, platforms, APIs, developer tools, ecosystems, or partner integrations
- Took a product or platform from ambiguous opportunity through launch, adoption, and evolution
- Deep technical fluency: APIs, authentication and authorization, distributed systems, platform boundaries, integrations, data flows, reliability, security trade-offs
- Built for developers, technical users, AI-native workflows, or platform ecosystems; direct experience with AI agents, MCP, agent frameworks, agentic coding tools, or agentic developer platforms strongly preferred
- A clear point of view on building for human developers versus AI agents (APIs, documentation, tooling, evaluation, observability, quality)
- Strong partner and customer instincts; aligns teams without authority; balances speed, security, quality, enterprise requirements
- Regular hands-on use of AI tools in own work

**Nice to have:** partner platforms, marketplaces, developer ecosystems, cloud platforms, databases; OAuth, delegated access, API governance, partner billing, provisioning, multi-tenant models; MongoDB or data infrastructure.
