## About the role (quoted)

"You will own the strategy and roadmap behind the consumer-facing experience that patients use to access care through our enterprise partners. This is a B2B2C role: our patients interact with Wheel through partner brands, so you'll need to design experiences that satisfy a consumer's expectations for a modern healthcare product while also meeting the technical, brand, and compliance requirements of large enterprise partners."

"You'll operate at a systems level, connecting patient-facing experience decisions to the underlying clinical, operational, and platform architecture that makes those experiences possible. You bring real hands-on experience shaping AI-powered product, not just using AI tools in your own workflow. This is a builder role for a senior product leader who can set a multi-year vision for patient experience and drive it forward through close, hands-on partnership with Engineering."

## Responsibilities

Strategy & Vision
- Define the multi-year vision and roadmap for the patient experience across Wheel's white-labeled consumer surfaces, balancing partner-specific requirements with a coherent, scalable core product
- Establish a point of view on where the patient experience should be standardized across partners versus configured per partner, and build the frameworks to support that decision-making
- Translate enterprise partner requirements across market segments into a unified product strategy
- Champion design (the posting calls it a strategic differentiator, a tag Daniel does not claim); work with Design on "what a trustworthy, AI+human powered patient experience looks like at scale"

Systems Thinking & Cross-Functional Execution
- Map the patient journey end to end, from first touchpoint through clinical encounter to follow-up; find friction, drop-off, and trust gaps
- Partner with Engineering, Clinical Operations, and partner-facing teams on solutions that hold up across a distributed clinician network and multiple partner integrations
- "Define the product requirements, success metrics, and evaluation criteria for AI-driven capabilities within the patient experience (for example, intake, triage support, or personalization), including partnering on model and product tradeoffs and on iteration post-launch based on real usage data"
- Break ambiguous cross-functional problems into requirements and plans; drive alignment with Engineering from concept through launch

Cross-Functional Leadership
- Primary product voice with Account Management and enterprise partners on patient experience
- Communicate vision, tradeoffs, and progress to executives, engineers, and non-technical partner stakeholders
- Mentor and influence other PMs on systems thinking and product craft, in partnership with their managers
