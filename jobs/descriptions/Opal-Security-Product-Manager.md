## Company

Opal Security is a cybersecurity company with offices in New York City and San Francisco, founded in 2020, 51-200 employees, Series B, $56.8M raised, backed by Greylock and Battery Ventures. Customers range from hypergrowth startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Product: AI-native identity and access management (IAM).

## Responsibilities

- Own one of two swim lanes:
  - Define Opal's agentic security strategy: how enterprises govern AI agents' access
  - Build AI-powered agents that deliver customer outcomes
- Spend about half the time with enterprise customers and internal go-to-market teams
- Spend the other half on product planning: requirements, KPIs, and 2-week to 6-month roadmaps
- Work daily with engineering on sprint scope and priorities
- Decide whether to kill, pivot, or double down on initiatives based on customer feedback and metrics
- Communicate strategy and trade-offs to stakeholders in writing and presentations
- Grow toward a potential successor role to the CPO

- Mid-to-senior PM with 5+ years building and scaling technical B2B products
- 0-to-1 launches and turning around underperforming products
- Comfortable with enterprise customers and with engineering on technical architecture

## Nice to have

## Company diligence (October 18, 2026)

**Product.** AI-native access governance: "the access control plane for every identity" (humans, non-human identities, AI agents). OpalQuery (plain-English access queries), OpalScript (approval rules as code), Paladin (AI agent that approves safe requests, escalates, revokes). Opal Zero launched September 17, 2026 for least-privilege AI agents: inventories agents, Paladin evaluates requests, Gateway Sync writes scoped time-bound grants into the customer's MCP gateway; deepest integration is with Claude (Compliance API, Claude Code admin controls, Enterprise-Managed Authorization for MCP connectors); $30K first-year launch offer. Agents in Opal (AI-guided access reviews) announced June 16, 2026.

**Developer surface.** docs.opal.dev has a 195-page API reference across ~27 resource groups (resources, groups, requests, campaigns, Paladin agents, event streams, tokens, non-human identities), a CLI, a Terraform provider, webhooks for request changes, and an llms.txt index. Mature for a company this size; a real API product to own.

**Customers.** Databricks (86K just-in-time requests), Notion, Cloudflare, Scale AI, CoreWeave, Superhuman, Elastic, Chronosphere, Mercari, Instacart (quoted on Opal Zero), Faire.

**Funding and leadership.** $22M Series B December 2023 (Battery, Greylock, Box Group). $23M in June 2026 led by the same insiders plus Cambium Capital, total $59M. No new lead and a round the same size as the B two and a half years later reads as a reset round. Howard Ting (ex-Cyberhaven CEO, Greylock EIR; earlier Palo Alto Networks, Nutanix, RSA) became CEO December 2025. Co-founder and former CEO Umaimah Khan is not on the leadership page; her exit is not explained publicly. Co-founder Stephen Cobbe is not on the leadership page either. CTO Alex Pien promoted internally (ex-Meta). CPO Sameer Mehta joined June 2026 from Veza, a direct competitor. Five senior hires in June 2026; "more than 60% of the workforce joined in 2026."

**Size.** Aggregators put headcount between 36 and 62; Clera says 51-200. 21 open roles on October 18, 2026, 10 of them sales. All non-sales roles are San Francisco. Benefits page: hybrid schedule, unlimited PTO, 100% medical for dependents.

**Culture signals.** Glassdoor 3.8 from 16 reviews, 65% recommend. Pros: dedicated technical team, mentorship, impact. Cons: lean team, turnover, startup strain. Two May 2025 reviews allege fake five-star reviews. Thin and partly contested evidence.

**Market.** Identity governance for AI agents is contested: SailPoint (Harbor Pilot, Agent Identity Security), Veza, ConductorOne, plus Okta's Cross App Access. Opal is the smallest well-funded entrant and is betting on MCP-gateway integration and the Claude ecosystem.

**Fit verdict.** Strong product match, weak logistics match. The posting is hybrid San Francisco (3 days required, 5 preferred), not remote, which fails the Boise relocation floor at this band. The CPO succession pitch is real in the listing ("the CPO's number two," "potential successor to the CPO") but the CPO is four months in. Revised score 65.
