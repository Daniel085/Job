The Safe Access role describes a product I have spent the last year on the developer-facing side of: a centralized access system that every other product depends on, used by first-party systems, direct API customers, and third parties, where the developer's first experience of it decides whether the controls get used or worked around.

Crux sits in the call path. It applies identity and policy before any AI is invoked, plays and logs consent in the network, then hands the call to a virtual agent or streams the media to a post-call intelligence partner. It is an access system by construction, and the stakes are the same as yours: get it wrong and a third party hears a conversation it should not. The platform and its access model were designed before I joined. What I built is how developers get access to it: developer.alianza.com, where a partner learns the platform's resource model, runs the eligibility check, the per-account connection with its on/off gate, and assignments scoped to an account, user, or phone number, and finds the authentication guide they integrate against, with every page generated by AI agents and reviewed by humans. I helped guide the structure of the Crux API with Architecture, and with Architecture I set the standards engineering teams build to and review their proposals, which is the part of this job description about building new programs on shared primitives rather than forking them. The same surface serves third-party AI experience providers, the operators whose networks carry the calls, and Alianza's own provisioning systems. It shipped as the 1.0 developer experience in September 2026.
